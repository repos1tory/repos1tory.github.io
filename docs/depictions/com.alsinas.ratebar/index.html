RateBar

battery flow, at a glance

Adds one signed number beside the iPad status bar battery percentage. It is the net power going into or out of the battery, in watts.

+6.2W   26%   [battery]
-2.1W   84%   [battery]
 0W    100%   [battery]
  • + — energy is going into the battery
  • - — the battery is powering the iPad
  • 0W — full on power, or negligible flow

what the number means

The value is calculated from battery current and voltage. It is not the charger's advertised wattage: the charger first runs the screen, CPU, Wi-Fi and everything else, and the number is what is left over for the battery. A 15 W charger can therefore show +4W while the iPad is busy.

The sign is the useful part: positive while charging, negative while draining, even when the iPad is plugged in but using more power than the adapter supplies.

notes

To make room, the status icons immediately left of the battery percentage (Wi-Fi, rotation lock, Focus) are shifted left by the width of the number. They are restored if the label hides. The tweak only reads battery state; it does not control charging.

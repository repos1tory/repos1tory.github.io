RateBar

battery flow and time remaining, at a glance

Adds one signed number and an estimated time beside the iPad status bar battery percentage. The number is the net power going into or out of the battery, in watts. The time is until full while charging, or until empty while draining.

+6.2W 3h30m   26%   [battery]
-3.1W 1h07m   12%   [battery]
 0W           100%   [battery]
  • + — energy is going into the battery
  • - — the battery is powering the iPad
  • 0W — full on power, or negligible flow
  • time — until full when positive, until empty when negative

what the number means

The value is calculated from battery current and voltage. It is not the charger's advertised wattage: the charger first runs the screen, CPU, Wi-Fi and everything else, and the number is what is left over for the battery. A 15 W charger can therefore show +4W while the iPad is busy.

The sign follows the actual battery current. If the iPad is plugged in but the load is higher than the charger can supply, the number goes negative and the time shown is time to empty, because the battery really is draining.

time estimates

  • Draining: uses iOS's own time-remaining estimate when available, otherwise remaining mAh ÷ current.
  • Charging: remaining mAh ÷ current charge current. It updates as the charge rate tapers, so the estimate can get longer as the battery fills.
  • These are estimates. A heavy app, brightness change, or thermal throttle can move the drain estimate quickly.

notes

The label uses the stock status bar font, size and colour. To make room, the status icons immediately left of the battery percentage (Wi-Fi, rotation lock, Focus) are shifted left by the exact width of the rate and time; the shift is recalculated when the text changes or an icon appears. They are restored if the label hides. The tweak only reads battery state; it does not control charging.
